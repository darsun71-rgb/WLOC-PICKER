WLOC changes Apple’s network (Wi-Fi / cell) location, not the GPS chip. Works best indoors / on Wi-Fi. Outdoors with strong GPS, the real position can still win — check the blue dot in Apple Maps after a restart if it doesn’t update.

Use it in 30 seconds

No install. Keep this page on your Home Screen for one-tap access.

  1. Pick the spot — tap the map, drag the pin, search, use your current location, or paste numbers / an Amap link above.
  2. Check the system — if the numbers came from Amap choose GCJ-02, from Baidu choose BD-09. Otherwise leave WGS84.
  3. Copy & paste — use the Loon or Shadowrocket copy button, then paste into the module’s arguments. Restart the phone if Apple Maps still shows the old dot.
